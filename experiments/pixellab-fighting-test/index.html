Quality Assessment

Based on Idle/Rotation Images (Steps 1 complete, 2-5 pending)

✓ Character design: Clearly reads as a green robot — good silhouette with sci-fi armor plating and glowing accents. Color identity is strong (green metal with green energy highlights).


✓ Proportions: Heroic preset worked — wider shoulders, thick legs. Feels like a fighting game fighter rather than a generic character.


✓ Outline quality: Clean single-color outline, detail holds at 128px. This is the sweet spot for 16-bit sprite work — visible detail, not overly noisy.


⚠ SF2 comparison: The idle pose lacks the "weight" and tension of a real SF2 idle. Template skeleton drives the pose, so it defaults to a neutral stand rather than an aggressive fighting stance. "fight-stance-idle-8-frames" template should help this once frames arrive.


⚠ Side view artifacts: At "side" view, the south/north-facing rotations look top-down-ish rather than pure side-scroll. For a fighting game you'd only use east/west and they look solid.
